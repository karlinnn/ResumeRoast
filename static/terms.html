What ResumeRoast Is

ResumeRoast is an AI-powered resume feedback tool. It analyses your resume against a job description and produces a score, roast commentary, and—for paid users—rewritten bullet points and a cold email draft.

No Guarantees

Scores, rewrites, and all other outputs are estimates generated by an AI model. They are intended as a starting point for reflection, not as guarantees of ATS compatibility, interview callbacks, or job offers. Results may vary and should not be treated as professional career advice.

Verify All Rewritten Content

You are solely responsible for reviewing and verifying any rewritten or improved resume content before using it in a real job application. Do not submit AI-generated text without reading and editing it to ensure it accurately reflects your own experience and skills.

Upload Only Your Own Resume

By uploading a document you confirm that it is your own resume and that you have the right to process it. Do not upload resumes belonging to other individuals. Uploading third-party personal data without consent may violate applicable privacy laws.
