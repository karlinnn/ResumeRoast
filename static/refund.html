Refund Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What You're Buying

The paid report is a digital, one-time product priced at the amount displayed at checkout. There is no subscription, no recurring charge, and no hidden fee.

When We Will Refund or Regenerate

If your payment succeeds but the report fails to generate or cannot be delivered to you (e.g. due to a server error or AI provider outage), we will either:

  • Regenerate your report at no additional cost, or
  • Refund the full amount to your original payment method.

We will do our best to resolve issues promptly once you contact us.

When We Will Not Refund

Once a report has been successfully delivered, no refund will be issued. Because the product is a digital report generated on demand, delivery constitutes fulfilment of the order.

How to Request a Refund

Send your refund request via the Contact page within 7 days of your payment. Please include your payment reference or report ID so we can locate your order quickly.
