Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Your Resume & Job Description

Resumes and job descriptions are not stored on our servers. When you submit your resume, it is sent directly to an AI provider to generate your score and roast. The data is held in memory for up to 30 minutes so that a paid report can be generated if you choose to unlock one. After that window, it is discarded.

What We Do Store

We store the following:

  • Payment status – whether a report has been paid for, linked to a one-time report ID (not to your identity).
  • Anonymous usage events – page views, score generated events, and similar aggregate analytics. These do not contain resume content or personally identifiable information.

Payments

Payments are processed by Razorpay. We do not see or store your card details at any point. All card and bank data is handled directly by Razorpay on their secure infrastructure. Please review Razorpay's Privacy Policy for details on how they handle payment data.

AI Provider

Resume text and job descriptions are transmitted to a third-party AI provider (Google Gemini) for processing. We do not share this data with any other parties. The AI provider's own privacy and data-use policies apply to data sent to their service.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us via the Contact page.
